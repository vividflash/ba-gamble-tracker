# BA Gamble Tracker

Records the rewards from Barbarian Assault low and medium gambles in the Loot
Tracker.

RuneLite records high gambles already, so all three tiers appear in the Loot
Tracker panel. Rewards that drop to the floor on a full inventory are counted
too. There is nothing to configure.

Gambling repeatedly is fine: rewards are matched to gambles in the order they
arrive, and a high gamble takes its own reward out of the way rather than leaving
it for the next one. Two rewards that land on the same tick cannot be told apart,
so they go into one record and the other gamble records nothing.
